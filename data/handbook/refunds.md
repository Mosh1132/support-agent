Refunds are allowed only for delivered orders.
Refunds over $100 need manager approval before they are issued.
Orders that are already refunded or cancelled cannot be refunded again.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Orders that have not shipped can be cancelled at no charge.
Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs extra.
You can return an item within 30 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging.
Customers pay return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.
If an item arrives damaged, contact support within 7 days with your order ID.
